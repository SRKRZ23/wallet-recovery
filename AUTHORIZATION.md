> relevant jurisdiction adapt it before use. Its whole purpose is to make the engagement provably
> *owner-authorized* — the one thing that keeps recovery legal and a real business.

We will attempt recovery of **these wallet(s) only**, identified by their public address. We will not

## 3. Ownership / authorization attestation (Client)
The Client attests, under their own responsibility, that they are **the lawful owner** of the wallet(s)
above, **or** a person legally authorized to recover them (e.g. executor/heir with documentation,
or an officer of the owning entity), and will provide reasonable evidence on request. Typical evidence:
the remembered secret itself deriving to the stated address; prior transactions the Client can explain;
for estates, letters of administration / probate.

**If ownership cannot be substantiated, we decline and terminate — no exceptions.**

## 4. How the work is done (data handling)
- The Client provides **only partial information** (the remembered part of the key/passphrase/seed) plus
  the **public address**. The Client is advised **never to share a full private key or full seed** with
  anyone, including us.
- For sensitive cases the recovery tool is **run on the Client's own machine** (it is open and offline),
  so the complete secret is assembled only under the Client's control. We never take custody of funds.
- On success, the **Client** receives the recovered secret and moves the funds themselves.

## 5. Fees — no recovery, no fee
- **Free feasibility assessment** first. If a search is physically infeasible, we say so and charge nothing.
- On successful recovery, a **success fee of ______ %** of the recovered value, payable **only** upon
  successful recovery. No recovery → no fee. Exact percentage agreed here, in writing, before work begins.

## 6. No guarantee; scope refusals
Recovery depends entirely on how much the Client genuinely remembers. We make **no guarantee** of success.
We will refuse and terminate if: ownership cannot be shown; the request concerns a wallet the Client does
not own; or the Client asks us to scan, enumerate, or access third-party wallets. **We do not sweep funds
that are not the Client's.** If a weakness in someone else's wallet is ever discovered, the correct action
is responsible disclosure to that owner — never a sweep.
